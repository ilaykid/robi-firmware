HeyRobi — Browser Setup & Customization Wizard

Open-source AI voice robot for kids • Direct to Gemini Live • Zero server • No software install

🔒 100% Local in Browser • Zero Data Sent to Any Server
🛡️
Total Privacy Guarantee — Zero data is sent to us, and nothing is ever stored by us!
This page is a static, client-side browser tool (Client-Side WebSerial with no backend server and no database). Your Gemini API key, WiFi credentials, and kids' names are never sent to the project authors or this site, never pass through any third-party server, and are not even saved in your browser's localStorage — they are flashed directly from your browser over USB-C into your HeyRobi's internal flash chip at home.

Step 1 — Direct Connection to Google Gemini

Your robot connects directly over home WiFi to Google Gemini Live — with zero relay server in between! Your API key is stored exclusively inside HeyRobi's local memory at home.

How to get a free API key in 30 seconds:
  1. Open Google AI Studio — API Keys and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Create API key and copy the key starting with AIza....
  3. Paste it below and click Verify Key to confirm it works.
🔒 Zero tracking & zero storage: Your key is never sent to this site or stored in your browser — it is verified directly against Google and flashed over USB-C straight into your HeyRobi.
Google's Free Tier includes roughly ~45 minutes of active net voice talk time per day (up to 500 sessions / 1,000,000 tokens, resetting daily at Google). You can monitor estimated daily usage in HeyRobi's local Parent Portal (http://robi.local).

Step 5 — Home WiFi & 1-Click USB-C Flash!

Connect HeyRobi to your computer with a USB-C cable. One click flashes HeyRobi's firmware and your personal settings directly to the chip!
🔒 Your WiFi credentials live in your browser RAM only — they are never sent to this site or saved anywhere outside your HeyRobi.

Note: ESP32-S3 connects to 2.4GHz WiFi networks (leave blank if you prefer HeyRobi to open a Robi-XXXX hotspot for phone setup).
